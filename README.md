# HR-Analytics-dashboard
Power BI dashboard providing insights into employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and HR performance metrics.

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze employee attrition trends and workforce demographics. The dashboard provides HR stakeholders with actionable insights into employee turnover, job satisfaction, age distribution, and educational background to support data-driven retention strategies.

## 🎯 Business Objective

Employee attrition can negatively impact organizational productivity, increase recruitment costs, and reduce workforce stability.

This project aims to:

- Analyze employee attrition patterns
- Identify departments with high turnover
- Understand attrition across age groups and education fields
- Evaluate job satisfaction levels across job roles
- Support HR decision-making through data-driven insights

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. High Overall Attrition

The organization recorded an attrition rate of **16.12%**, indicating a significant level of employee turnover.

### 2. Departmental Turnover

Research & Development and Sales departments contributed the highest number of employee exits.

### 3. Age Group at Risk

Employees aged **25–34 years** experienced the highest attrition, suggesting challenges in retaining early-career professionals.

### 4. Education Background Trends

Employees from **Life Sciences** and **Medical** fields accounted for the largest share of attrition.

### 5. Job Satisfaction Patterns

Several job roles displayed lower satisfaction distributions, potentially contributing to higher turnover rates.

---

## 📌 Recommendations

### Improve Employee Retention Programs

Develop targeted retention initiatives for departments experiencing higher attrition.

### Enhance Career Growth Opportunities

Provide mentorship, learning programs, and promotion pathways for employees in the 25–34 age bracket.

### Conduct Regular Employee Surveys

Gather employee feedback regularly to identify workplace concerns before they result in resignation.

### Monitor High-Risk Employee Segments

Track turnover trends among specific departments, age groups, and educational backgrounds.

### Review Workload Distribution

Assess workload balance and management practices in departments with elevated attrition levels.
